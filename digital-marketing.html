Digital Marketing

The channels, ideas and habits behind modern online marketing, explained for beginners.

What is digital marketing?

Digital marketing means promoting a business, product or idea through online channels such as search engines, websites, email and social media. The aim is the same as any marketing: reach the right people, give them a reason to care, and make the next step easy.

What makes it different is that it is flexible and easier to learn from. You can try a small idea, see how people respond, and adjust. That makes it a good fit for beginners and small budgets, as long as you stay focused.

New here? Start with our beginner's guide to digital marketing for small businesses. To understand how people find you through search, visit SEO & Growth. For ideas on what to publish, see Content & Social Media.

  • Start with the audience

    Who are you trying to reach, and what do they need from you?

  • Fewer channels, done well

    One or two channels you can keep up beat six you abandon.

  • Learn from results

    Look at what people actually do, then improve one thing at a time.

The essentials

The main digital marketing channels

Most online marketing is built from a handful of channels. You do not need all of them at once.

  • Search (SEO)

    Helping your pages appear when people search for what you offer. Learn about SEO.

  • Content marketing

    Publishing useful articles, videos or guides that earn attention and trust over time.

  • Social media

    Building an audience and conversations on the platforms where your customers spend time.

  • Email marketing

    Staying in touch with people who have chosen to hear from you.

  • Paid advertising

    Paying to put your message in front of a chosen audience. It is usually best tested with a small budget first.

  • Your website

    The home base every channel should lead to, so keep it clear, fast and easy to use.
