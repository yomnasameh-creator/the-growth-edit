How to Create a Marketing Plan for a Small Business

A step-by-step framework for setting goals, choosing channels and building a simple plan you can actually follow.

1. Clarify Your Offer and Goal

Start by defining what you sell, who benefits most from it, and one primary goal for the next 30 to 90 days—such as getting inquiries, building awareness, or driving product page visits.

2. Define Your Target Customer

Write down the specific problem your customer faces, what they look for before buying, and where they spend attention online or locally.

3. Choose One or Two Focused Channels

Instead of trying every platform at once, pick the channels that fit your time, budget and strengths—such as helpful search content, social media posts, or direct outreach.

4. Set a Simple Weekly Routine and Review

  • Publish consistent, helpful content rather than posting only when you feel inspired.
  • Track basic actions like clicks, messages, sign-ups or sales.
  • Review what worked each month and refine your next steps.
