SEO for Beginners: How Search Engines Work

Learn how search engines find, store and rank pages, so everything you publish has a better chance of being discovered.

1. Crawling: How Search Engines Discover Pages

Search engines start by finding pages through links across the web and through your XML sitemap. If a page cannot be reached by links or returns a broken status, search engines struggle to discover it.

2. Indexing: How Pages Are Stored and Understood

Once a page is fetched, search engines analyze its text, headings, title tags, meta descriptions and structure to understand what the page is about and decide whether to store it in their index.

3. Ranking: How Results Are Ordered

When someone searches, the search engine looks for indexed pages that match the intent of the query, are clear and helpful, and offer a smooth experience on mobile and desktop.

Practical Steps You Can Take Today

  • Use one clear main heading (H1) and logical subheadings (H2, H3) on each page.
  • Write unique, descriptive title tags and meta descriptions that match the page content.
  • Keep internal links working and update your sitemap whenever you add or change pages.
