About the platform

A practical guide to marketing and growth

THE GROWTH EDIT is an educational platform for people who want to understand how modern marketing works, without the jargon. Every guide is written in plain English and focuses on steps you can apply to a real project, whether that is a first blog, a local shop or a personal brand.

New to the subject? Start with the digital marketing basics, learn how SEO works, then explore branding and business fundamentals. You can also read more about the project.
