SEO & Growth

How search engines work, and how to help the right people find your website.

What is SEO?

SEO stands for search engine optimization. It is the work of making your website easier for search engines to understand, and more useful for the people who find it. When done well, it helps the right visitors discover your pages when they search for something you can help with.

SEO is not a trick or a switch. It is a steady habit of building a clear website, publishing helpful content and fixing problems as you find them. Nobody can promise a specific ranking, and results usually take time, so patience matters.

Start with our guide SEO for Beginners: How Search Engines Work. SEO also works best alongside good content and the wider digital marketing picture.

  • Technical foundations

    A fast, mobile-friendly site with clean links that search engines can read.

  • Helpful content

    Pages that answer the questions people are actually asking.

  • Trust over time

    Quality, consistency and reputation build slowly, but they last.

The essentials

Core SEO concepts

Learn these six ideas and most SEO advice becomes much easier to follow.

  • Crawling

    Search engines use automated programs to discover pages by following links from one page to another.

  • Indexing

    After a page is found, the search engine analyzes it and may store it so it can appear in results.

  • Ranking

    When someone searches, the engine decides which stored pages are most relevant and useful, and orders them.

  • Keywords and search intent

    Keywords are the words people type. Intent is the reason behind them: to learn, compare, buy or find a place.

  • On-page SEO

    Clear titles, descriptions, headings, readable content and descriptive image text on each page.

  • Technical SEO

    Site speed, mobile layout, a sitemap, clean URLs and no broken links.
