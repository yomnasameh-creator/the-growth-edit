About THE GROWTH EDIT

An independent educational platform that explains digital marketing and business growth in plain English.

Why this site exists

Marketing advice online is often rushed, full of jargon, or written for people who already know the basics. THE GROWTH EDIT takes a different approach. It explains one idea at a time, in everyday language, and shows how each idea connects to the rest.

The site is an independent learning project. It is built to share useful, beginner-friendly knowledge and to put good SEO practice into action on a real website.

Who it is for

  • Digital marketing beginners who want a clear starting point instead of a hundred open tabs.
  • Small business owners who need practical ideas for finding and keeping customers.
  • Brand builders who want to shape a recognisable identity, voice and message.

What you will find here

The articles are organised into five topics. Each topic page collects related guides in one place:

  • Digital Marketing: the main channels and how they work together.
  • SEO & Growth: how search engines work and how to help people find your pages.
  • Content & Social Media: planning and publishing content that is worth reading.
  • Branding: identity, voice and positioning.
  • Business Basics: customers, offers, pricing and planning.

If you are not sure where to begin, read Digital Marketing for Small Businesses: A Beginner's Guide.

Our editorial approach

  • Clear before clever. Short sentences, everyday words, one idea at a time.
  • Practical over theoretical. Guides end with steps you can try on your own project.
  • Honest about limits. We do not publish invented statistics, fake reviews or made-up results. If we cannot back up a claim, we leave it out.

A note on advice. The content on this site is educational and general. Every business is different, so test ideas on a small scale and adapt them to your own situation.
