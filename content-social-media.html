Content & Social Media

How to plan, create and share content that people actually want to read, watch and pass on.

Why content matters

Content is how a brand speaks: articles, videos, images, emails and social posts. Good content answers real questions, shows what you know and gives people a reason to come back. It also feeds your other channels, since search engines and social platforms both need something worth showing.

Social media is where much of that content gets shared and discussed. The best approach for beginners is simple: choose the platforms your audience actually uses, publish at a pace you can keep up, and pay attention to how people respond.

Content works best when it connects to a clear digital marketing plan, supports your SEO, and speaks in a consistent brand voice.

  • Be useful first

    Help the reader solve a problem before you ask for anything.

  • Be consistent

    A steady rhythm you can sustain beats bursts of activity.

  • Be human

    Plain language and a clear point of view stand out from generic posts.

The essentials

Building blocks of a content strategy

  • Content pillars

    Three to five themes you return to again and again, so your content feels focused rather than random.

  • Content calendar

    A simple schedule of what you will publish and when, so you are never starting from a blank page.

  • Formats

    Articles, short videos, carousels, newsletters and more. Choose formats you can produce well.

  • Platform fit

    Each platform has its own style and audience. Adapt your message instead of posting the same thing everywhere.

  • Repurposing

    Turn one idea into several pieces, such as an article that becomes a short post and an email.

  • Review and improve

    Look at what people read, save and share, then make more of what works.
