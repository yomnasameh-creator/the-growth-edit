Branding

How to build a brand that is clear, consistent and easy to remember, starting with the basics.

What is a brand?

A brand is more than a logo. It is the overall impression people have of your business: what you stand for, how you speak, how you look and how it feels to deal with you. Every post, page, email and conversation adds to that impression.

For a small business, strong branding does not require a big budget. It requires clear decisions: who you serve, what makes you different, and how you want to sound and look. Once those are decided, the rest becomes much easier to keep consistent.

Branding connects closely to your content and social media choices and to the basics of your business, such as your audience and your offer.

  • Clarity

    People should understand what you do and who it is for within seconds.

  • Consistency

    The same voice, colors and message everywhere builds recognition.

  • Authenticity

    A brand that matches how you really work is easier to maintain and trust.

The essentials

The building blocks of a brand

  • Audience

    Who you are speaking to, what they need and what matters to them.

  • Positioning

    Where you sit among alternatives, and the reason someone would choose you.

  • Brand promise

    A simple statement of what people can expect every time they deal with you.

  • Brand voice

    The personality of your words: friendly, direct, expert, playful, or a blend.

  • Visual identity

    Your logo, colors, typography and imagery, chosen to work together.

  • Brand guidelines

    A short reference document so anyone creating for your brand makes the same choices.
