Privacy Policy

A short, plain-language explanation of how this website handles information.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version

THE GROWTH EDIT is a static educational website. It does not have user accounts, a database or a server of its own, and it does not collect, store or sell personal information through its pages.

Information we do not collect

  • The site does not create accounts or store logins.
  • The forms on the site are front-end design previews. Anything typed into them stays in your browser and is not sent or saved. See the contact page and the newsletter section on the home page.
  • The site does not use advertising trackers or sell data.

Hosting and server logs

The website is delivered by a third-party hosting provider. Like most hosts, the provider may automatically record technical information when a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages visited. This is used to run and secure the service. We do not control this data, so please read your host's own privacy information for details.

Fonts from Google Fonts

To display its typography, this site loads the Space Grotesk and Inter fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, which means Google may receive your IP address and basic browser details. You can read how Google handles this in its own privacy documentation.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own. Third-party services used to deliver it, such as the hosting provider or font service, may use technical data as described above.

Analytics

At the time of writing, no analytics tool is installed on this site. If one is added in the future, this page will be updated to explain what is measured and why.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to external websites, such as social media profiles. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other sites, so we recommend reading their policies before sharing any information.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles information changes, for example if a working newsletter or analytics is added, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, please visit the contact page or read more about the project.
